About Us

We built the fraud team that small businesses couldn't hire.

MeraFraud started from a simple observation: enterprise-grade fraud detection has always existed — but only enterprises could afford it. SMEs were left to fend for themselves against the same sophisticated attacks.

Our mission

Give every small and medium e-commerce business access to the same real-time, machine-learning-driven fraud protection that only large platforms could previously justify building in-house — without the enterprise price tag, the months-long integration, or the black-box decisions.

What We Believe

Our principles

Explainability over black boxes

A risk score without a reason isn't useful — it's just another number to distrust. Every decision MeraFraud makes comes with plain-language reasoning.

Access, not exclusivity

Fraud protection shouldn't be a luxury reserved for companies with dedicated risk teams. We designed MeraFraud to be usable by a two-person shop on day one.

Your rules, not just ours

No two stores have the same risk tolerance. That's why thresholds are configurable per merchant, not fixed by us.

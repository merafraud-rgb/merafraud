FAQ

Common questions

How does the 7-day free trial work?

Every plan — including Starter — starts with a full 7-day free trial. If you don't cancel before day 8, billing starts automatically at the plan's listed price.

What happens if I go over my monthly transaction limit?

We'll reach out before any disruption to your service — you won't be cut off automatically mid-month.

Can I change my risk thresholds later?

Yes, at any time via the API or your dashboard — there's no need to contact support to adjust sensitivity.

Do you accept Turkish Lira / local Turkish cards?

Yes. Payments are processed via a local Turkish payment provider, so Turkish-issued cards and TRY billing are fully supported.

Is my transaction data used to train models for other customers?

See our Privacy Policy for details on data handling and model training practices.

What are your support response times?

Starter includes self-serve chat and documentation with no formal SLA. Growth includes email support with a target first response under 1 business day. Scale includes priority support with a target first response under 4 business hours via a dedicated channel. See our status page for current system status.
